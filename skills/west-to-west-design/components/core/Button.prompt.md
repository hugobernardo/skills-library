Brand button — primary (Dark Ocean fill; Flag Green fill on dark grounds) or outline, 14px/600 Inter, 6px radius, −1px hover lift.

```jsx
<Button variant="primary" href="#join">Join Us</Button>
<Button variant="outline">Learn More</Button>
<Button variant="primary" tone="dark">Join Us</Button>
```

`tone="dark"` for buttons sitting on Ink/Dark Ocean sections (hero, newsletter strip). Secondary CTAs may end with " →".
