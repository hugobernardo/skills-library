Section opener — uppercase eyebrow with a 24px Flag Green tick, Sora H2 in Title Case, optional 18px slate subtitle.

```jsx
<SectionHeader eyebrow="What We Offer" title="How We Help Ventures Succeed" subtitle="From your first steps in the Valley to building a lasting network, we're here at every stage." />
```

`dark` flips colors for Ink sections (green eyebrow, white title). `center={false}` for left-aligned variants.
