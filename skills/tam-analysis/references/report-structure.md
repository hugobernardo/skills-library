# Research report and investor explanation structure

The Source of Truth is the system of record; the research report is the *argument* behind it and the investor explanation is a *view* of it. Produce the report in SIZE mode (and the reconciliation report in RECONCILE mode) alongside the artifacts; produce the investor explanation whenever the number will be used externally.

## Industry overview (per market, before any sizing)

Explain the industry in plain English:

- **Market definition** — what the category is
- **Customer** (who pays) and **end user** (who benefits) — often different
- **Value chain** — map the participants (e.g., Manufacturer → Distributor → Retailer → Systems → Infrastructure → Platform → Consumer); adapt per market
- **Economics** — who captures revenue, how businesses charge, pricing models, gross-margin structure, major cost centers, switching costs, procurement cycles
- **Industry structure** — fragmentation vs concentration, major players, channel power, technology maturity, regulation, structural trends
- **Market evolution** — what changed in the last 5–10 years; what will change in the next 5–10; separate structural trends from hype
- **The company's role** — what it replaces, what it complements, what it enables that was previously uneconomic, who benefits, who loses economic power

## Research report (Parts I–XII)

**I. Executive summary** (≤2 pages): the opportunity in plain English · headline table (Market | TAM | SAM | 5Y SOM | Confidence) · most important conclusion (strongest evidence-supported thesis) · most important caveat (what investors should *not* conclude) · largest strategic opportunity · largest strategic risk.

**II. Market architecture**: diagram of how the markets relate; classify each as core / adjacent / dependent / future optionality.

**III. Market deep dives** — identical structure per market: 1 overview · 2 industry structure · 3 value chain · 4 customer problem · 5 company's role · 6 bottom-up TAM · 7 top-down TAM · 8 reconciliation · 9 TAM/SAM/SOM · 10 bear/base/bull · 11 competitive landscape · 12 advantages · 13 disadvantages · 14 key risks · 15 key assumptions · 16 evidence gaps · 17 confidence score · 18 market verdict.

**IV. Competitive landscape**: competitive map · competitor table · right-to-win · where competitors are better · platform/disintermediation risks · future competitive scenarios.

**V. TAM reconciliation**: master table (Market | Bottom-Up | Top-Down | Defensible TAM | SAM | 5Y SOM | Confidence) with discrepancy explanations.

**VI. TAM overlap analysis**: overlap map; additive / partially additive / monetization layer / mutually exclusive; adjusted total — or an explicit recommendation *not* to present a single number if it would mislead.

**VII. Investment-committee red team**: ≥5 objections, each argued at full strength and then adjudicated.

**VIII. Final verdict**: venture-scale answer · most important market today · greatest long-term upside · what to deprioritize · 5–10 milestones for 12–24 months.

**IX. Investor explanation** (see below).

**X. Assumption register** · **XI. Source ledger** · **XII. Research gaps**: what we know · what we think · what we do not know · what we need to validate, prioritized by decision impact, with the fastest validation method for the top five (customer / distributor / partner interviews, pricing tests, partner data, sales experiments).

## Investor explanation

- **30-second version** — the TAM without jargon: who the buyers are, roughly how many, what they pay, and the range.
- **2-minute version** — the underlying market · how the bottom-up works · how it cross-checks top-down · the initial SAM · where expansion comes from.
- **Investor FAQ** — answer the questions this market invites, typically: Why isn't this just [incumbent category]? Why won't [platform] build or bundle it? Why do customers need this? Why isn't TAM just "count × $X"? What share is reachable? Which assumptions are weakest? How does ARPU expand? Are revenue streams double-counted? Why venture-scale rather than a niche business?

Every number in the investor explanation must be in the "Claims cleared for external use" table with the same wording and status.

## Executive memo (≤2 pages, all modes)

TL;DR with ranges · what was found · the conflicts or sensitivities that mattered and how each was resolved · what the red team changed · what to do with it (lead with X, never show Y, validate Z next) · where the analysis is still weak, stated plainly · deliverables list.
